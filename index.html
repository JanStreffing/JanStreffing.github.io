01Models

2019 → today

AWI-CM3

OpenIFS 43r3 atmosphere coupled to the multi-resolution FESOM2 ocean and sea ice through OASIS3-MCT. Runs from 61 km / 25–125 km CMIP-class setups up to 9 km storm-resolving projections. Described in Streffing et al. 2022.

DocumentationDescription paperSource

2024 → CMIP7

AWI-ESM3

AWI-CM3 extended to an Earth system model: OpenIFS 48r1, FESOM 2.7 with REcoM biogeochemistry, LPJ-GUESS dynamic vegetation and the PISM ice sheet via SCOPE. Running the CMIP7 historical now; designed so that the same executable can later carry glacial climates.
